### 🩺 Library Doctor

A nightly health check for **Plex, Jellyfin and Emby** with Radarr and Sonarr. It finds missing files, duplicates, broken files, wrong audio languages and stuck downloads, with a button to fix most of them. Nothing is deleted without asking. Free and open source, currently in beta.

**[Try the live demo](https://librarydoctor.uk/demo/)** · [Install it](https://librarydoctor.uk/install/) · [Code](https://github.com/spikey1965/library-doctor) · [Discord](https://discord.gg/rmuKypgFgB)
